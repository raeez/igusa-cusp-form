\part{The Igusa comparisons}
\chapter{The denominator and the reduced counting series}
The Jacobi form \(\phiweak\) supplies the automorphic input.
The type-II Lorentzian lattice, positive roots, Weyl vector, and
Gritsenko--Nikulin presentation supply the denominator algebra.
These data give
\[
 \den(\gIg)=\Dmon(2Z),\qquad
 \Dtheta=64\Dmon,\qquad \ChiTen=\Dmon^2.
\]
On the root torus, Chevalley chains of \(\nIg\) have Euler
supercharacter
\[
 P=\prod_{\alpha>0}(1-e^{-\alpha})^{\sdim\gIg_\alpha},
 \qquad e^{-\rho}P=\den(\gIg).
\]
PBW gives \(\sch\Fock(\nIg)=P^{-1}\). For \(\jmath(Z)=Z/2\),
\[
 -\jmath^*\!\left(e^{2\rho}
    \sch\bigl(\Fock(\nIg)^{\tensor2}\bigr)\right)
 =-\ChiTen^{-1}.
\]
The primitive reduced curve-counting theorem identifies its
numerical generating series with this scalar, in the specified
normalization and expansion domain.

\chapter{The maps needed for compact realization}
The scalar comparison retains signed dimensions. A compact
realization begins with reduced coefficient complexes on moduli
of objects of \(S\times E\). It requires their common-torsor
extension and flag correspondences and coherent orientation maps.
The Hall product acts on the resulting state complex. Its primitive
classes must satisfy the Igusa presentation.
Theorem~\ref{igusa:primitive-presentation-map} constructs the
primitive comparison map once these classes and relations are
given. It also proves the parity-refined PBW criterion for this map
to be an isomorphism.

The coproduct and pairing must be compared before identifying
quantum doubles. A chiral realization preserves position, support,
and collision operations. A Pfaffian realization requires a family
of skew complexes and an isomorphism of its square-root line with
the pullback of \(\mathcal L^5\tensor\nu_5\).
This isomorphism must preserve the section and its transport.
An operator realization also specifies its Hilbert or cochain
carrier and the domain on which its normalized trace converges.

The diagonal Fredholm construction gives an explicit analytic model
on its stated virtual root superspace. Its determinant is the
Borcherds product on the absolute-convergence chamber. Relating
this model to compact states requires an intertwiner and the line
comparison above. The full construction problem is to produce
compatible maps on geometric states, Hall operations, chiral
operations, and square-root lines. Their scalar evaluation must
recover the established Igusa identity.
