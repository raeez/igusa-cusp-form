the compact `K3xE` Hall source.

`FINITE_HALL_CONILPOTENT_SOURCE_COALGEBRA_OBSTRUCTION_VERIFIED` is an

named and that the present worktree does not supply the coalgebra rows,
filtration rows, conilpotence rows, transition rows, inverse-limit rows,

Ran formalism, and primitive or pairing-kernel lim1 ledgers are not
