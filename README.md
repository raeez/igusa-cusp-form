# The Igusa Square Root

Raeez Lorgat

Let S be a smooth projective K3 surface and E an elliptic curve. Reduced curve counting on S × E, in classes primitive and nonzero in the K3 factor, produces the reciprocal of the monic Igusa cusp form χ₁₀. Its monic square root is an automorphic section of weight five with an order-two character. A geometric realization asks for extension operations on states, their parity, an orientation line, and a comparison of its section with that square root.
